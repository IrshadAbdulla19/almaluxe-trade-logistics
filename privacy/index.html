Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Almaluxe Export LLP ("we") handles information you submit through this website.

Information we collect

When you send an enquiry we collect the details you provide: name, phone number, email, company, country or city, the product or service you are interested in, and your message. We may also record the advertising campaign that brought you to the site (for example, UTM tags) and the date of your enquiry.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotes, and follow up about our products and services.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. It may be processed by service providers we use to run our business, such as hosting, spreadsheets and messaging tools.

Lead ads

If you submit a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, Meta shares the details you entered in that form with us.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your information by contacting info@almaluxeexportllp.com.
